What is msCNVS & Sequmed msCNV Web App?

An introduction to the technology and its clinical value

Overview

The Sequmed CNV Web App is an online analysis platform built on msCNVS (medium throughput single-cell CNV sequencing). It enables clinical and research users to analyze single-cell copy number variations (CNVs) with high accuracy, low cost, and minimal technical barriers.

Core Value of msCNVS

Traditional single-cell CNV sequencing (scCNV-seq) often suffers from high cost, amplification bias, and dependence on specialized instruments. msCNVS overcomes these limitations through three major innovations:

  • No pre-amplification (preWGA) — msCNVS uses a modified Tn5 transposase to directly label single-cell genomes, eliminating the bias introduced by whole-genome amplification.
  • Medium throughput — Supports 48–384 single cells per run using standard microplates. With dual indexing, the theoretical upper limit reaches 18,432 uniquely identifiable cells.
  • Low sequencing cost — Requires only 1–3 Gb per cell, which is 10–20× less than PTA or MALBAC, making it suitable for routine clinical applications.

Advantages of the Sequmed msCNV Web App

  • No bioinformatics background required — The platform automates the entire workflow, from data upload to CNV visualization.
  • Broad sample compatibility — Supports amniotic fluid cells, blastocysts, circulating tumor cells (CTCs), and patient-derived xenograft (PDX) nuclei.
  • High accuracy — CNV results show strong concordance with clinical gold standards such as chromosome microarray analysis (CMA) and G-banding karyotyping (correlation \( R > 0.9 \)).
